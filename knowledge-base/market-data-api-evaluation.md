# Market-data API evaluation

Documentation review and first authenticated Alpaca paper-account probe: **2026-10-08**; FMP free-key access check: **2026-10-12**. This compares provider documentation with the [accepted requirements](decision-tree.md) and [workflow design](workflows.md). The authenticated results below establish only the tested endpoint behavior; they do not establish after-hours behavior, data rights, or a final provider selection.

## Required fit

- A dated catalog of current S&P 500 traded securities and sourced sectors.
- Five years of daily OHLCV plus the close preceding the earliest selectable session.
- One cumulative, provisional current-day bar refreshed about hourly during regular US hours and checked after close while the provider still updates that day's bar, with source and as-of time.
- Provider daily-bar price/volume conventions, a consistent feed, and a defensible dollar-volume input. The accepted volume follows the provider's same-day bar and may include extended-hours trades.
- Zero monthly data cost for the creator's private homelab; a separately licensed path for later trader-facing use.

## Alpaca Basic: official-documentation fit

**Documented** describes published capability or limitation, not a successful account probe. **Probe** means a product-critical behavior or entitlement remains unresolved.

| Requirement | Finding | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Five years of daily prices | Basic lists historical US stock/ETF data **since 2016**, a latest-15-minute historical restriction, and 200 historical calls/minute. The requested span, including a preceding close, fits on paper. [Plan](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api). | Documented; probe coverage and quality. |
| Five stocks × five years per backfill activity | `/v2/stocks/bars` accepts comma-separated symbols, `1Day`, and `limit` up to 10,000 **total** bars per page. It may return fewer than the limit and sorts by symbol before timestamp. Follow every `next_page_token`. About 6,300 expected bars could fit one page, but one page is not guaranteed. [Historical bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars). | Documented; probe pages, latency, bytes, retries. |
| Full-market delayed feed | Basic gives real-time **IEX only**; historical SIP can be queried when `end` is at least 15 minutes old. Specify `feed=sip` for history and do not silently combine IEX and SIP. The FAQ shows material volume differences. [Plan](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), [FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq). | Documented. |
| Hourly cumulative bar for today | Historical `end` defaults to 15 minutes ago without real-time entitlement, but the endpoint does not promise a cumulative current-day `1Day` bar. [Multi-symbol snapshots](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stocksnapshots-1) document `dailyBar`, `prevDailyBar`, and `feed=delayed_sip`; the [FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq) says latest endpoints require subscription for `feed=sip`. Neither statement establishes Basic entitlement to **delayed** SIP snapshots or their cutoff behavior. | Probe historical `1Day`, then delayed SIP snapshots. |
| Provider daily bar with after-hours volume | Alpaca's [trade-condition rules](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq) say extended-hours conditions `T` and `U` can **increase the same daily bar's volume** without updating its prices. The user accepted following this changing volume in the provider's daily candle. Minute bars apply different extended-hours price rules. | Documented aggregation rule; probe when revised values become available through Basic. |
| Daily VWAP and exact traded notional | The [FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq) shows `vw` in bar examples. VWAP uses trades updating both high/low and volume; `v` can include other trades. The documented daily bar does not include exact traded notional. `v × vw` is therefore an **estimate**, not exact all-trade notional. | VWAP documented; exact notional unverified. |
| Adjustments and ticker changes | Historical bars support `raw`, `split`, `dividend`, `spin-off`, `all`, and comma-separated combinations. `asof` maps tickers across a rename; the FAQ says new historical mapping is available the day **after** a rename. Return basis remains [deferred](https://github.com/maorts14/market-eagle/issues/3). [Endpoint](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars), [FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq). | Documented; probe splits and renames. |
| Private and later commercial use | Published [Terms and Conditions](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/TermsAndConditions.pdf) describe personal, non-commercial use and a notice requirement before making a user application available to others. The [customer agreement](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/AcctAppMarginAndCustAgmt.pdf) restricts reproducing, distributing, selling, or commercially exploiting market data without written consent. Applicable account terms and derived-display rights need confirmation. | Private use appears plausible under applicable terms; trader-facing use is **not cleared**. |

The [accepted dollar-volume rule](decision-tree.md) prefers provider-reported traded notional, then `shares × daily VWAP`, then `shares × daily close`. Both fallbacks require an estimate label. Alpaca's VWAP denominator can differ from its bar volume, so the VWAP product must never be presented as exact traded notional. [Issue #4](https://github.com/maorts14/market-eagle/issues/4) tracks provider verification and any change to this rule.

## FMP for constituents and sectors

Alpaca bars do not establish S&P 500 membership or sectors. FMP publishes a [current constituent endpoint](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/sp-500) with symbol, name, sector, sub-sector, and `dateFirstAdded` in its sample response. This documents the **response shape**, not free-plan access, complete/correct current membership, or an official GICS taxonomy. The [S&P DJI index page](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/sp-500/) distinguishes companies from traded share lines.

FMP's [pricing page](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/pricing) describes Basic as free with 250 calls/day and end-of-day data. Endpoint access cannot be inferred from a general plan description. On **2026-10-12**, one authenticated read-only `stable/sp500-constituent` request with the user's free key returned **HTTP 402**. The same key returned **HTTP 200 and eight rows** for documented `stable/search-symbol?query=AAPL`, so the key works generally; the constituent endpoint is specifically unavailable to this account. This is an account-specific entitlement observation, not a claim that every free key or future plan behaves identically. No response payload or secret was retained in the repository. The pricing page also says displaying or redistributing FMP data needs a separate agreement.

## No-cost current catalog options and private decision

**No reviewed $0 source currently establishes all three at once: exact current S&P 500 membership, per-security sectors, and permission for automated use in this app.** A public download is evidence of availability, not permission to build and distribute a derived catalog. The options below preserve the distinction between an official index, a fund's holdings, and a community-maintained list.

**Accepted for the first private version (2026-10-12):** prioritize a working real-data app with a dated, manually checked public S&P 500 list as the provisional catalog. The [Wikipedia table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_S%26P_500_companies) is the first input candidate because it has symbol and sector columns; the operator must record the exact page revision and review its applicable terms before import. Cross-check every proposed membership/sector change against dated [S&P DJI announcements](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/indexnews/announcements/20260306-1482263/1482263_march2026rebalance1546.pdf) and [IVV](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239726/ishares-core-sp-500-etf)/[SPY](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy) holdings; record source dates and differences, and hold unresolved identities or sectors for manual review. ETF portfolios are cross-checks, not an index-member feed. Save a dated immutable snapshot with source URL/revision, retrieval and review times, sector taxonomy, share-class mapping, and a **provisional** membership/sector label. Publish it manually only after the review, then show that label and as-of date in the explorer and agent answers.

The existing ingestion code and scheduled-admission gate are still tied to `fmp-sp500`; implementation must add a distinct provisional source ID and explicit private-mode eligibility. Do not relabel public-list data as FMP, automatically publish a held catalog, or let an unreviewed list authorize full-universe collection. This decision applies only to the creator's private access; it does not establish automated reuse or public/trader-facing display rights. A certified licensed source is [for later, issue #57](https://github.com/maorts14/market-eagle/issues/57), and stronger catalog update/accuracy and rights work is [for later, issue #58](https://github.com/maorts14/market-eagle/issues/58).

## Other free-plan screening

The earlier 2026-10-06 comparison was a published-plan screen, not an authenticated benchmark: [Twelve Data Basic](https://twelvedata.com/pricing) has tight per-minute/daily credits for repeated 500-symbol refreshes; [Massive Stocks Basic](https://massive.com/pricing) advertises two historical years and end-of-day access; [Alpha Vantage free](https://www.alphavantage.co/support/) has 25 requests/day and premium US delayed/real-time data. None currently provides a documented replacement for the full first-release workload. Recheck entitlements before switching candidates.

## Authenticated implementation probe and provider-selection gate

### First paper-account observation, 2026-10-08

Using an Alpaca paper key, the read-only probe in `scripts/probe_alpaca.py` requested raw SIP daily bars for AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, GOOG, and JPM across roughly five years. At 17:39 UTC during the US regular session, the request succeeded with **1,257 bars per symbol**, about **708 KB** of JSON, and no next-page token. Two observed response times were about **1.5 and 6.1 seconds**. The rate-limit response header showed 199 remaining after each request; this is an observation, not a throughput guarantee.

An authenticated historical `1Day` request ending 20 minutes before the probe returned a current-day bar for all five symbols. A separate `feed=delayed_sip` snapshot request also returned `dailyBar` for all five. Their same-day `t` fields were the same 04:00 UTC session anchor. Their close and volume values differed at this instant, consistent with distinct delayed cutoffs; **`t` is not a publication or finality timestamp**. The snapshot's `latestTrade.t` was about 15 minutes behind probe time, but that trade timestamp is not itself the daily bar's as-of time. Collection must retain its own `fetched_at` and mark the as-of kind as fetched unless the provider supplies a bar-specific publication timestamp.

This probe confirms that the paper account can obtain the requested five-year SIP history and a provisional current-day daily bar through both routes. It does **not** yet confirm same-day after-hours volume revisions, correction behavior, split/rename quality, full-universe throughput, or redistribution rights. FMP free-key constituent access was separately checked and returned HTTP 402. A later-session repeat should compare the same session's volume and OHLC at regular close, after hours, and the following day. Credentials are only in ignored local configuration.

1. With a Basic key, request five years of `1Day` `feed=sip` bars for five symbols with `limit=10000`. Follow all page tokens; record counts, bytes, latency, 429/rate headers, and invalid-symbol behavior. Include a preceding close, split, rename, and narrow stock/date repair. No key is stored in this repository.
2. During and after a regular session, compare historical `1Day` ending at least 15 minutes ago with multi-symbol snapshots using `feed=delayed_sip`. Record Basic entitlement, cumulative-bar behavior, after-hours volume revisions to the same dated bar, timestamps/as-of semantics, and coverage at refresh ticks. Use a bounded after-close check window and refetch the prior session later; do not require proof of an exact provider-final time. If neither endpoint works, probe time-filtered minute-bar aggregation and its API cost before selecting it.
3. Compare daily `v`/`vw` for liquid and thin stocks, including pre/post-market activity. Verify that the chosen dollar-volume estimate and displayed source/as-of details match the provider's bar convention; do not label native daily volume regular-session-only.
4. Treat FMP's free-key constituent HTTP 402 as an access blocker for this account. If an authorized source becomes available, review membership, share classes, sector taxonomy, update time, request limits, and terms before approving its first catalog.
5. Review applicable agreements and obtain the needed rights before trader-facing or other multi-user deployment. An API success or paid individual subscription alone does not establish redistribution rights.

**Decision state:** Alpaca Basic remains the leading documented private-development **price** candidate. A manually reviewed public-list catalog is accepted for the first private version; FMP's constituent endpoint is blocked for this free account, and the code has not yet been changed to import/publish the provisional source. After-hours bar behavior, full-universe verification, and private source/usage review remain implementation gates. Certified catalog data and trader-facing rights are for later. Exact publication/finality timing is not a gate: the product retains as-of times, checks after close, and refetches older bars for corrections. The provider's daily-volume convention is accepted for the first definition.
